## Which makeup service lasts the longest?

HD Makeup and Airbrush Makeup are designed to provide long-lasting wear for extended events.

---

## Can I bring reference photos?

Yes.

Clients are welcome to bring inspiration photos so our beauticians can better understand their preferred style.

---

## Can I bring my own makeup products?

Yes.

If you have preferred makeup products due to personal preference or skin sensitivity, please discuss this with your beautician before your appointment.

---

## Which makeup service is best for photography?

HD Makeup and Airbrush Makeup are excellent choices for professional photography because they provide smooth, camera-friendly finishes.

---

## How long does a makeup appointment take?

The duration depends on the selected service and the complexity of the desired look. Our staff can provide an estimated appointment time when booking.
