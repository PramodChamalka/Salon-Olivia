# Keratin Treatment

## Category

## Description

Keratin Treatment smooths hair, reduces frizz, and improves shine without permanently changing the hair structure.

## Price

LKR 20,000 – 45,000

## Suitable For

- Frizzy hair

## Benefits

- Improved shine

- Smoother texture

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I wash my hair immediately after treatment?

Your beautician will advise you on the recommended waiting period and aftercare based on the treatment used.

### Is keratin the same as rebonding?

No. Keratin smooths and nourishes hair, while rebonding permanently changes the hair structure.
