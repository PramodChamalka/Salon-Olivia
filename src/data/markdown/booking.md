# Appointment Booking

## Overview

Salon Olivia accepts both appointments and walk-in clients.

Appointments are recommended to reduce waiting time and ensure your preferred beautician and time slot are available.

---

## Booking Methods

Clients can book appointments through:

- Salon Olivia website
- Telephone
- Visiting the salon in person

---

## Walk-In Appointments

Walk-in clients are welcome.

Services will be provided based on beautician availability.

Waiting times may vary during busy periods.

---

## Bridal Bookings

Bridal services should be booked well in advance.

Certain bridal packages may require:

- Initial consultation
- Trial session
- Deposit confirmation

---

## Home Bridal Services

Home bridal services are available in selected areas.

Additional travel charges may apply depending on the location.

---

## Booking Multiple Services

Clients may book multiple services during one appointment.

Examples include:

- Haircut + Facial
- Hair Color + Hair Spa
- Spa + Manicure + Pedicure
- Bridal Makeup + Hair Styling

Booking multiple services in advance is recommended.

---

## Arrival Time

Clients should arrive 10–15 minutes before their appointment.

This allows sufficient time for:

- Consultation
- Service preparation
- Registration (if required)

---

## Late Arrivals

Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may:

- Receive a shortened service
- Be asked to reschedule
- Lose their appointment if insufficient time remains

---

## Cancellation Policy

Clients should notify Salon Olivia at least 24 hours before cancelling or rescheduling.

Repeated last-minute cancellations may require advance payment for future appointments.

---

## Same-Day Appointments

Same-day appointments may be available depending on staff availability.

Clients are encouraged to call before visiting.

---

## Booking Recommendations

Salon Olivia recommends booking early for:

- Bridal Makeup
- Bridal Packages
- Engagement Makeup
- Weekend appointments
- Public holiday periods
- Festive seasons

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended but not mandatory.

Walk-ins are accepted based on availability.

---

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes.

Please inform the salon at least 24 hours in advance.

---

### Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes.

Advance notice of at least 24 hours is appreciated.

---

### Do you accept same-day bookings?

Yes.

Availability depends on the salon schedule.

---

### How early should I book bridal services?

As early as possible.

Bridal services are in high demand and should be reserved several weeks or months before the wedding date.

---

### Can I book multiple services together?

Yes.

Our team will arrange a schedule to accommodate multiple services during the same visit whenever possible.
