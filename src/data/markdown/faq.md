# General Information

## What is Salon Olivia?

Salon Olivia is a ladies-only beauty salon offering professional hair, beauty, makeup, nail, spa, facial, bridal, and special occasion services.

## Where is Salon Olivia located?

Sri Lanka

## What are your opening hours?

Monday – Saturday

9:00 AM – 7:00 PM

The salon is closed on Sundays unless announced otherwise.

## Is Salon Olivia a ladies-only salon?

Yes.

Salon Olivia provides beauty and wellness services exclusively for ladies and girls.
