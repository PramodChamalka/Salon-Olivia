# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I combine multiple services?

Yes.

Many clients combine makeup, hairstyling, and nail services for a complete beauty experience.

---

## Should I book early?

Yes.

Weekend appointments and special event bookings are often in high demand. Booking early helps secure your preferred date and time.

---

## Can I request a specific style?

Yes.

You are welcome to bring inspiration photos or discuss your preferred style with our beauticians during your consultation.

---

## How long does event styling take?

The duration depends on the services selected. Our team can provide an estimated appointment time when you book.

---

## Do you offer group bookings?

Yes.

Salon Olivia can accommodate group bookings for events such as weddings, graduations, family celebrations, and corporate functions. Advance booking is recommended.

---

# Important Information

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to allow time for consultation and preparation.

If your event includes multiple participants or requires several beauty services, we recommend contacting Salon Olivia well in advance so our team can prepare accordingly.

Prices may vary if additional services or customized styling requests are added to the booking.
