# Nail Care Aftercare

After your nail service, Salon Olivia recommends:

- Moisturizing your hands and cuticles regularly.
- Wearing gloves when using harsh cleaning chemicals.
- Avoiding excessive force on nail extensions.
- Scheduling maintenance appointments as recommended.
- Returning to the salon for professional removal of gel or acrylic enhancements.

---

# Important Information

Salon Olivia follows strict hygiene and sanitation procedures.

All nail tools and equipment are thoroughly cleaned and sanitized between clients to ensure a safe and comfortable experience.

If you have any nail infections, allergies, or skin conditions affecting your hands or feet, please inform your beautician before your appointment.
