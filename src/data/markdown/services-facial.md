# Facial Aftercare

After your facial treatment, Salon Olivia recommends:

- Drink plenty of water.
- Avoid touching your face unnecessarily.
- Avoid heavy makeup immediately after treatment where possible.
- Use sunscreen during the daytime.
- Follow any skincare advice provided by your beautician.
