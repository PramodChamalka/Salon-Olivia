# Salon Olivia Policies

## Overview

Salon Olivia is committed to providing a safe, relaxing, and professional beauty experience for every client. The following policies help ensure excellent service, fairness, and comfort for all visitors.

---

# Ladies-Only Policy

Salon Olivia provides beauty and wellness services exclusively for ladies and girls.

Male visitors may accompany clients to the reception area but are not permitted inside treatment or service areas.

Salon Olivia reserves the right to refuse services that violate this policy.

---

# Appointment Policy

Appointments can be made:

- Through the official website
- By telephone
- By visiting the salon

Walk-in customers are welcome based on staff availability.

Clients are encouraged to book appointments in advance, especially for:

- Bridal services
- Weekend appointments
- Holiday periods
- Special event services

---

# Arrival Policy

Clients should arrive 10–15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

Early arrival allows time for:

- Consultation
- Registration
- Service preparation

---

# Late Arrival Policy

Clients arriving within 15 minutes of their appointment will be accommodated whenever possible.

Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may experience one of the following:

- Reduced treatment time
- Appointment rescheduling
- Appointment cancellation if sufficient time is unavailable

---

# Cancellation Policy

Clients should provide at least 24 hours' notice when cancelling or rescheduling an appointment.

Repeated last-minute cancellations or missed appointments may require advance payment for future bookings.

Bridal and special event bookings may require longer notice periods.

---

# Bridal Booking Policy

Bridal bookings may require:

- Initial consultation
- Trial session
- Deposit payment

Deposits are generally non-refundable unless cancellation complies with salon policies.

Package details should be finalized before the appointment date.

---

# Payment Policy

Salon Olivia accepts:

- Cash
- Debit Cards
- Credit Cards

Full payment is due immediately after services are completed unless otherwise agreed.

Prices may change without prior notice.

---

# Refund Policy

Salon Olivia does not provide cash or card refunds for completed beauty services.

If a client is dissatisfied with a service, they should contact the salon within 48 hours.

After review, Salon Olivia may provide:

- Complimentary touch-up
- Service adjustment

Beauty products may only be exchanged if they are defective or damaged at purchase.

---

# Health & Safety Policy

Clients should inform their beautician about:

- Allergies
- Sensitive skin
- Pregnancy
- Medical conditions
- Previous reactions to beauty treatments

Salon Olivia maintains strict hygiene standards by sanitizing equipment after every client.

Clients with contagious illnesses may be asked to reschedule their appointment.

---

# Children Policy

Children receiving services must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Children who are not receiving services should remain supervised throughout their visit.

---

# Personal Belongings

Clients are responsible for their personal belongings.

Salon Olivia is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal items.

---

# Photography Policy

With client permission, Salon Olivia may photograph completed beauty work for:

- Website
- Social media
- Promotional materials

Clients may decline photography at any time.

---

# Respectful Behaviour

Salon Olivia is committed to providing a respectful environment.

Abusive, threatening, or inappropriate behaviour toward staff or clients will not be tolerated.

The salon reserves the right to refuse service when necessary.

---

# Privacy Policy

Personal information collected during appointments is used only for:

- Appointment management
- Client communication
- Service records

Salon Olivia does not sell or share personal information except where legally required.

---

# Policy Updates

Salon Olivia reserves the right to update these policies at any time.

The latest version will always be available through the salon.
